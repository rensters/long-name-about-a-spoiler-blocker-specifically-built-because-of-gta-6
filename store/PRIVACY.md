# Privacy policy: i can't afford a PS5 but i also wish to not get spoiled

This extension does not collect, send, sell or share any data. At all.

- Everything runs inside your browser. The extension makes no network requests of its own: no servers, no analytics, no crash reports, no remote keyword lists, no update checks.
- It reads the text of the pages you visit (titles, posts, channel names, image descriptions) only to decide, on your device, whether to blur something. That text is never stored or sent anywhere.
- Your settings and keyword packs are saved in your browser's own extension storage. If you have browser sync turned on (Chrome/Brave/Edge/Firefox Sync), your browser syncs them between your own devices. That's your browser's sync, and the extension has no access to where it goes.
- The toolbar badge count and the "things blurred on this page" number are calculated on the spot and never leave your device.

Permissions and why they're needed:

- **Read and change data on all websites**: to find and blur spoilers on the sites you visit.
- **Storage**: to save your settings and keyword packs.
- **Alarms**: for the "pause for 10 minutes" timer and the switch-off date reminders.
- **Notifications**: to remind you before a pack switches itself off on the date you picked.
